# BrainSilencer / Noise Inverted Metronome
Noise generator with an inverted metronome

**Live demo:** https://evgenytsitsorin.github.io/BrainSilencer/  

## What is an BrainSilencer?

BrainSilencer is an experimental sound tool designed for people who experience a constant stream of thoughts, mental chatter, or difficulty quieting their minds.

Instead of adding more sounds, BrainSilencer uses rhythmic moments of silence within continuous background noise. The brief interruptions create a subtle auditory pattern that gives your attention something external to follow.

Choose White, Pink, or Brown Noise, adjust the rhythm and silence duration, and find a sound pattern that feels comfortable to you.

No complicated exercises. No instructions to clear your mind. Just sound, silence, and a different way to direct your attention.

BrainSilencer is an experimental auditory attention tool, not a medical treatment or a guaranteed way to stop unwanted thoughts.

You don't have to silence your mind. Give it something else to listen to.
## Features

- White, Pink, and Brown Noise.
- Custom sound mixing with adjustable frequency bands.
- Adjustable rhythm from 30 to 240 BPM.
- Silence intervals from 0 to 1000 ms.
- Real-time sound customization.


## Browser support

Current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari (anything with `AudioWorklet` support).
