A different way to direct your attention

Does your mind keep talking when you want to relax, focus, or fall asleep? BrainSilencer offers a different kind of listening experience for moments when you feel caught in a constant stream of thoughts.

BrainSilencer is an experimental auditory attention tool for people who experience mental chatter, racing thoughts, or difficulty switching off their inner monologue.

How does it work?

Instead of marking each beat with a click, an inverted metronome briefly interrupts continuous background noise. These short gaps create a subtle rhythm for your attention to notice and follow.

Choose White, Pink, or Brown Noise, or shape your own sound with the frequency mixer. Adjust the tempo and silence duration to find a pattern that feels comfortable to you.

No complicated exercises and no pressure to clear your mind. Just sound, silence, and another place to rest your attention.

You don’t have to silence your mind. Give it something else to listen to.

BrainSilencer is an experimental auditory attention tool, not a medical treatment or a guaranteed way to stop unwanted thoughts.

Frequently asked questions

Can BrainSilencer help with overthinking?

BrainSilencer is designed as an experimental auditory attention tool. Its rhythmic pauses may give you something external to notice and follow, but individual experiences vary. It is not a treatment for overthinking or any mental health condition.

Does BrainSilencer stop unwanted thoughts?

No. BrainSilencer does not switch off thoughts or control mental activity. It offers a sound pattern that you can choose to focus on when you want to shift your attention.

Can I use it before sleep or while working?

You can experiment with BrainSilencer during quiet activities, work breaks, or as part of your bedtime routine. Choose a comfortable volume and stop using it if the sound feels distracting or uncomfortable.

Which noise is best for a busy mind?

There is no single best option for everyone. Try White, Pink, and Brown Noise at a comfortable volume, then adjust the tempo and silence length to find what you prefer.

What is the difference between white, pink and brown noise?

White noise has equal energy across all frequencies, so it sounds bright. Pink noise has less energy in the highs and sounds softer and more balanced. Brown noise is weighted toward the lows and sounds like a deep rumble.

Is anything recorded or uploaded?

The sound is generated locally in your browser with the Web Audio API and an AudioWorklet. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere, and there is no sign-up or account.

Why is there no sound on my device?

Browsers only start audio after you interact with the page, so press the play button first. Also check your system volume and make sure your browser supports AudioWorklet (all current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari do).
